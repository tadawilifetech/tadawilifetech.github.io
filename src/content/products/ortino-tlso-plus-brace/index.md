The Ortino TLSO PLUS provides complete support for the spine and the sternal (chest) region. A height-adjustable sternal pad, engineered locking straps and a precise pulley system deliver high stability without completely restricting movement. Use under your doctor’s supervision.

## Key Features
- **Adjustable sternal pad** — Height-adjustable chest pad that limits forward bending of the spine.
- **Easy-open fasteners** — Quick to put on and remove.
- **Ergonomic design** — Shaped to follow the structure of the body.
- **Precise pulley system** — Fine adjustment of compression.
- **Non-slip straps with engineered locks** — Secure fit that stays in place.
- **Lightweight, ventilated materials** — High stability without full immobilisation.

## Indications
- Recovery after thoracic or lumbar spine surgery
- Vertebral compression fractures
- Disc herniation and degenerative disc disease
- Spinal instability (e.g. spondylolisthesis)
- Spinal support for older adults or people with muscle weakness
- Spinal canal stenosis, especially thoracic

## Important Usage Tips
- **Consult your physician** — Speak with a specialist before starting any orthopedic support so it is used safely and effectively for your condition.
- **Limitations & warnings** — Follow the instructions carefully. Some conditions may not benefit from a brace, so a professional assessment matters.
- **Use intermittently** — Do not wear the brace continuously without breaks. Wear time depends on your condition and your doctor’s advice; remove it while sleeping unless instructed otherwise.
- **Keep exercising** — A brace supports you but does not replace strengthening exercises or physiotherapy recommended by your clinician.
- **Adjust correctly** — It should be firm enough to support, but never so tight that it restricts circulation. Pressure should be distributed evenly.
