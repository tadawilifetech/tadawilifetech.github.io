The Ortino LSO is a lumbosacral orthosis designed to support both the lumbar and pelvic regions. Its standard LSO back panel, modular side parts and pulley system make it suitable for more demanding conditions. Use it under your doctor’s supervision.

## Key Features
- **Advanced pulley system** — Easy, even tightening and a longer service life than elastic belts.
- **Standard LSO back panel** — Rigid polymer support designed to stabilise the spine.
- **Breathable polyurethane foam** — Reduces sweating and keeps the brace comfortable.
- **Adjustable, detachable side parts** — Modular design — the LSO can be converted to an LO when less support is needed.
- **Height adjustment** — The back panel height can be tuned for a better fit to your body.

## Indications
- Chronic low-back pain
- Herniated or bulging discs
- Severe strain of back muscles and ligaments
- Posture correction and muscular weakness or instability
- Osteoporosis and degenerative spine conditions
- Stabilising the lumbar and pelvic vertebrae during post-operative rehabilitation

## Important Usage Tips
- **Consult your physician** — Speak with a specialist before starting any orthopedic support so it is used safely and effectively for your condition.
- **Limitations & warnings** — Follow the instructions carefully. Some conditions may not benefit from a brace, so a professional assessment matters.
- **Use intermittently** — Do not wear the brace continuously without breaks. Wear time depends on your condition and your doctor’s advice; remove it while sleeping unless instructed otherwise.
- **Keep exercising** — A brace supports you but does not replace strengthening exercises or physiotherapy recommended by your clinician.
- **Adjust correctly** — It should be firm enough to support, but never so tight that it restricts circulation. Pressure should be distributed evenly.
