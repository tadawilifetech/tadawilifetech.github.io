Orthopedic Braces – FAQ

What is the difference between Ortino and Ortino Plus products?
Ortino products are general-use supports. Ortino Plus products are specialist orthoses that must be fitted and adjusted by an orthotist.
How are Ortino products different from other orthopedic supports?
Orthopedic supports fall into general products — mostly elastic and fabric, sold in pharmacies and offering less support — and specialist products. Ortino’s advanced range is specialist-grade, designed on the Aspen (USA) platform using premium polymer and textile materials.
What materials are Ortino products made of?
Advanced, unbreakable polyamide; non-stretch mesh and breathable fabrics; long-life Velcro; breathable Spanish polyurethane foam; and specialist-grade aluminium.
Should I see a doctor before buying a back brace?
Yes — if your back pain is acute or chronic, consult a specialist physician before using a brace.
How do I order?

Are Ortino products certified?
Ortino products hold ISO 13485, ISO 9001, CE marking and GMP certification.
