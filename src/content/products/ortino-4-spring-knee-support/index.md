The Ortino 4-spring knee support combines an ergonomic design with high-quality neoprene to give effective support to the knee joint, while increasing the feeling of stability and comfort during movement — ideal for everyday use and activity.

## Key Features
- **Premium neoprene** — Durable material with an abrasion-resistant outer layer.
- **Soft terry inner lining** — Keeps the joint warm and comfortable.
- **4 lateral reinforcing springs** — Stronger support for the knee.
- **Adjustable elastic Velcro straps** — An excellent, secure fit.
- **Light, ergonomic design** — Suitable for long-term wear; free size for most adults.

## Indications
- Reducing mild to moderate knee pain
- Improving stability in weak or unstable knees
- Protecting the knee during walking, sport and daily activities
- Preventing overload and injury
- Reducing stiffness by keeping the joint warm
- Active people, older adults and people who stand for long periods

## Important Usage Tips
- **Consult your physician** — Speak with a specialist before starting any orthopedic support so it is used safely and effectively for your condition.
- **Limitations & warnings** — Follow the instructions carefully. Some conditions may not benefit from a brace, so a professional assessment matters.
- **Use intermittently** — Do not wear the brace continuously without breaks. Wear time depends on your condition and your doctor’s advice; remove it while sleeping unless instructed otherwise.
- **Keep exercising** — A brace supports you but does not replace strengthening exercises or physiotherapy recommended by your clinician.
- **Adjust correctly** — It should be firm enough to support, but never so tight that it restricts circulation. Pressure should be distributed evenly.
