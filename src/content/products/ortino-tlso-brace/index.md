The Ortino TLSO is a complete brace supporting the thoracic (upper back), lumbar and pelvic regions. Its modular design combines rigid LSO and TLSO polymer panels with a cross-strap system. Use it under your doctor’s supervision.

## Key Features
- **Advanced pulley system** — Comfortable, precise adjustment with a long service life.
- **LSO and TLSO polymer panels** — Standard rigid support for the spine.
- **Breathable materials** — Less sweating and more comfort during long wear.
- **Cross-strap system** — Shoulder straps that support the upper back and encourage upright posture.
- **Modular design** — Can be converted to an LSO or LO brace as your recovery progresses.

## Indications
- Chronic back pain and spinal instability
- Disc herniation and protrusion
- Severe strain of back muscles and ligaments
- Degenerative spine conditions and osteoporosis
- Stabilising the thoracic, lumbar and pelvic vertebrae during post-operative rehabilitation

## Important Usage Tips
- **Consult your physician** — Speak with a specialist before starting any orthopedic support so it is used safely and effectively for your condition.
- **Limitations & warnings** — Follow the instructions carefully. Some conditions may not benefit from a brace, so a professional assessment matters.
- **Use intermittently** — Do not wear the brace continuously without breaks. Wear time depends on your condition and your doctor’s advice; remove it while sleeping unless instructed otherwise.
- **Keep exercising** — A brace supports you but does not replace strengthening exercises or physiotherapy recommended by your clinician.
- **Adjust correctly** — It should be firm enough to support, but never so tight that it restricts circulation. Pressure should be distributed evenly.
