The Ortino LO is a pulley-system lumbar brace built on the Aspen (USA) platform. “LO” stands for lumbar orthosis. It is light, slim and supportive — a good choice for people with chronic low-back pain, long sitting hours or physically demanding work.

## Key Features
- **Memory-foam back panel** — Adapts quickly to the shape of the lumbar spine for comfort and reduced pressure.
- **Advanced pulley system** — Unlike elastic belts that loosen over time, the pulley lets you tighten evenly and precisely without losing performance.
- **Discreet under clothing** — A slim ergonomic profile that fills the natural lumbar curve and can be worn under clothes.
- **Premium materials** — Durable Velcro straps and 3D breathable fabric for long-term wear.

## Indications
- Relief and management of chronic low-back pain
- Back support for office workers, drivers and people who sit or stand for long periods
- Support for people with osteoporosis by limiting sudden movements
- Reducing pressure from mild lumbar disc protrusion
- Injury prevention when lifting heavy objects

## Important Usage Tips
- **Consult your physician** — Speak with a specialist before starting any orthopedic support so it is used safely and effectively for your condition.
- **Limitations & warnings** — Follow the instructions carefully. Some conditions may not benefit from a brace, so a professional assessment matters.
- **Use intermittently** — Do not wear the brace continuously without breaks. Wear time depends on your condition and your doctor’s advice; remove it while sleeping unless instructed otherwise.
- **Keep exercising** — A brace supports you but does not replace strengthening exercises or physiotherapy recommended by your clinician.
- **Adjust correctly** — It should be firm enough to support, but never so tight that it restricts circulation. Pressure should be distributed evenly.
