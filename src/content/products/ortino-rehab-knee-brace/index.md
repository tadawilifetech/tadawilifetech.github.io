The Ortino graduated rehab knee brace is an excellent tool for supporting and rebuilding the knee. Its advanced adjustable hinge provides outstanding support during rehabilitation.

## Key Features
- **Ergonomic structure** — Shaped to follow the form of the knee.
- **Graduated hinge with lock** — Controls and limits the range of motion as prescribed.
- **Pulley pad** — Prevents the brace from slipping.
- **Light and flexible** — Comfortable for everyday rehabilitation.
- **Strong and durable** — Built for long-term use.
- **Durable elastic straps** — For a better, secure fit.

## Indications
- Cruciate and collateral ligament injuries (ACL, PCL, MCL, LCL)
- Rehabilitation after ACL or PCL surgery
- Chronic knee instability and muscle weakness
- Prevention of sports injuries
- Reducing pain from osteoarthritis and joint problems
- Knee stability in older adults

## Important Usage Tips
- **Consult your physician** — Speak with a specialist before starting any orthopedic support so it is used safely and effectively for your condition.
- **Limitations & warnings** — Follow the instructions carefully. Some conditions may not benefit from a brace, so a professional assessment matters.
- **Use intermittently** — Do not wear the brace continuously without breaks. Wear time depends on your condition and your doctor’s advice; remove it while sleeping unless instructed otherwise.
- **Keep exercising** — A brace supports you but does not replace strengthening exercises or physiotherapy recommended by your clinician.
- **Adjust correctly** — It should be firm enough to support, but never so tight that it restricts circulation. Pressure should be distributed evenly.
