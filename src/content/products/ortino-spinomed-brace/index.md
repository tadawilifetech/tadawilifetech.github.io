The Ortino Spinomed is a specialist spinal orthosis with a backpack-style strap system and a shapeable aluminium splint. It is light, ergonomic and designed to support an upright spine, especially in osteoporosis.

> This is an Ortino Plus specialist product. It must be used under the supervision of a specialist physician and fitted and adjusted by an orthotist.

## Key Features
- **Backpack-style strap system** — Distributes support across the shoulders and back for an upright posture.
- **Lightweight, ergonomic design** — Comfortable for daily wear.
- **Advanced aluminium splint** — Can be shaped by the orthotist to the patient’s spine.
- **Breathable, high-quality materials** — Comfort during extended use.

## Indications
- Osteoarthritis
- Osteoporosis
- General spinal support
- Supporting the spine when back muscles are weak

## Important Usage Tips
- **Consult your physician** — Speak with a specialist before starting any orthopedic support so it is used safely and effectively for your condition.
- **Limitations & warnings** — Follow the instructions carefully. Some conditions may not benefit from a brace, so a professional assessment matters.
- **Use intermittently** — Do not wear the brace continuously without breaks. Wear time depends on your condition and your doctor’s advice; remove it while sleeping unless instructed otherwise.
- **Keep exercising** — A brace supports you but does not replace strengthening exercises or physiotherapy recommended by your clinician.
- **Adjust correctly** — It should be firm enough to support, but never so tight that it restricts circulation. Pressure should be distributed evenly.
