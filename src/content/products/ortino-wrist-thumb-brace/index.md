The Ortino wrist–thumb brace reduces pain and inflammation and stabilises the wrist and thumb joints in conditions such as tendinitis, arthritis, carpal tunnel syndrome and movement injuries. It also helps prevent injury during daily, work and sports activities and supports recovery after surgery.

## Key Features
- **Two strong aluminium splints** — One supports the wrist, one stabilises the thumb.
- **Effective thumb stabilisation** — Controls painful thumb movement.
- **Light and comfortable** — Suitable for extended wear.
- **Right or left hand** — Flexible design fits either hand.
- **Daily and therapeutic use** — Supports work and recovery.

## Indications
- Reducing wrist and thumb pain and inflammation
- Carpal tunnel syndrome
- Tendinitis and thumb joint inflammation (e.g. De Quervain’s)
- Thumb arthritis and chronic pain
- Preventing injury from repetitive tasks (typing, phone use, industrial work)
- Stabilising the wrist and thumb after injury or surgery

## Important Usage Tips
- **Consult your physician** — Speak with a specialist before starting any orthopedic support so it is used safely and effectively for your condition.
- **Limitations & warnings** — Follow the instructions carefully. Some conditions may not benefit from a brace, so a professional assessment matters.
- **Use intermittently** — Do not wear the brace continuously without breaks. Wear time depends on your condition and your doctor’s advice; remove it while sleeping unless instructed otherwise.
- **Keep exercising** — A brace supports you but does not replace strengthening exercises or physiotherapy recommended by your clinician.
- **Adjust correctly** — It should be firm enough to support, but never so tight that it restricts circulation. Pressure should be distributed evenly.
