The Ortino unloader is an effective, non-surgical solution for knee osteoarthritis pain. By gently changing the alignment of the knee, it takes pressure off the worn compartment of the joint, reduces pain and makes walking easier and more natural.

## Key Features
- **Precise height adjustment** — Accurate positioning on the leg.
- **Advanced anti-slip suspension** — Stays in place while you move.
- **Fast Fit connectors** — Quick to put on and take off.
- **Specialised unloading straps** — Shift load away from the affected compartment.
- **Light, strong medical-grade aluminium** — Durable without extra weight.
- **Breathable polyurethane pads** — Reduce sweating for all-day comfort.

## Indications
- Reducing knee osteoarthritis pain
- Reducing pressure on the worn joint compartment
- Improving mobility and making walking easier
- Bow-legged (varus) knee alignment
- A safe, non-invasive option that may reduce reliance on painkillers

## Important Usage Tips
- **Consult your physician** — Speak with a specialist before starting any orthopedic support so it is used safely and effectively for your condition.
- **Limitations & warnings** — Follow the instructions carefully. Some conditions may not benefit from a brace, so a professional assessment matters.
- **Use intermittently** — Do not wear the brace continuously without breaks. Wear time depends on your condition and your doctor’s advice; remove it while sleeping unless instructed otherwise.
- **Keep exercising** — A brace supports you but does not replace strengthening exercises or physiotherapy recommended by your clinician.
- **Adjust correctly** — It should be firm enough to support, but never so tight that it restricts circulation. Pressure should be distributed evenly.
